New International Builder's Community Organization is a registered Cambodian NGO working in education and human-resource development across Siem Reap Province.

Contact the organization Registered with the Ministry of Interior, Kingdom of Cambodia
Our mission អំពីយើង

Sustainable community development and human-resource capacity building.

Our registered purposes include youth education, vocational and language training, early-childhood teacher development, parent counselling, library and educational publishing programmes, after-school and kindergarten provision, clean water and health education, and the expansion of social support networks — carried out in partnership with local institutions, provincial authorities and international collaborators.

What we run

កម្មវិធី

Rainbow Schools

Three kindergarten and after-school sites, staffed by teachers trained through our own early-childhood teacher-development course.

New International
Bethany Institute

A residential gap-year institute for young adults — English-language instruction, servant-leadership formation and vocational preparation.

Dreamville Edu Town

An integrated campus bringing our schools, training programmes and community facilities onto a shared site.
